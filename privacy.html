Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026 · Entity Solutions LLC

The short version. Housekept has no account and no login. Your home details, your maintenance log, your notes and your photos stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The answers you give about your home (home type, heating, water heater, gutters and so on).
  • The jobs you log: dates, costs, who did the work, notes, and any photos or receipts you attach.
  • Your own jobs, and any jobs you snooze, re-time or remove from your plan.
  • Your equipment and belongings: names, brands, model and serial numbers, install or purchase dates, prices, rooms, warranty dates, label photos, and any receipts, warranty cards, manuals or scanned pages you add.
  • Papers about your home that you add, such as an inspection report, insurance policy or closing documents.
  • Your quick reference notes (such as where the shutoffs are and paint colors) and their photos.
  • The pros you save: the names, phone numbers, email addresses and notes you type in.
  • Your settings, such as reminder time.

This data is stored only on your device. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, Apple includes it in that backup under your own Apple account. We can't access it.

What we collect

Nothing. Housekept has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party SDKs that collect data. The only time anything reaches us is if you choose to email support (see below).

Camera and photos

You can take photos with the camera or pick them with Apple's photo picker. Only the photos you take or pick are copied into the app, and they stay on your iPhone. When you photograph an appliance label, the text is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo and the text are never uploaded.

Documents and files

Pages you scan and PDF files you import (such as a manual) are copied into the app and stay on your iPhone.

Links to the web

Some buttons open a Google search in Safari, for example to find a manual, check for recalls or look up parts. When you tap one, Safari sends the search words (usually the brand and model number) to Google, the same as if you had typed them yourself. Housekept sends nothing on its own.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Housekept schedules them on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Calendar, Siri and search

Add to Calendar opens Apple's own event screen; Housekept does not read your calendar. If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Housekept, or search your iPhone, the information involved is handled on your device by Apple's system features under Apple's privacy policy.

Backups you export

Back up and restore makes a file with your Housekept data. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop), and it is only as private as the place you keep it. We never receive it.

Emailing us

If you write to support from the app, your email app sends us your message, your email address, and the Housekept and iOS version numbers so we can help. We use them only to answer you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions.

Deleting your data

Use Home, then Start over, to delete everything Housekept has stored: your home details, plan changes, logged jobs, equipment, belongings, home documents and other documents, quick reference and pros. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the device. Backup files you exported are yours to delete.

Children

Housekept is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
